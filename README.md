Website serves to preserve the Ngemba language. It is spoken in the Bamendjou village of Cameroon, and a few neighboring villages. This is a pictionary with 130 cards with images and words (French or English) that are clicked to play recordings of people in Cameroon saying the words themselves.

Word contributors: Estelle Catherine Matsingang, Jean Calvin, Maxime Nzongang, Pauline Matsindjou, Sylvie Mawe

Link: https://learnngemba.onrender.com/
